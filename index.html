No. 01

Tritonia Swim Central Pattern Generator

Rebuilt Getting et al.'s (1989) legacy NEURON/HOC model of a swim rhythm CPG in Python with Brian2. Led the modeling to test the rhythm's robustness against perturbations in stimulus strength and conductance, then ran a bifurcation analysis in XPPAUT to map equilibria and Hopf bifurcations. Co-presented at CAMP 2026.

Brian2XPPAUTComputational Neuroscience
No. 02

Zebra Finch Birdsong HVC Network Model

Reproduced Jin et al.'s (2007) network model of sequence generation by HVC neurons, then used it to investigate how intrinsic bursting and post-inhibitory rebound make the network robust to membrane noise. Co-presented at CAMP 2026.

Neural NetworksPython
No. 03

Modelling Brainwave Disorders Using Kuramoto Networks

Built Brian2 simulations of synchronization in coupled neural networks, presented at the Indian Academies of Science Summer School on Dynamical Systems & Complexity, and extended the work into neuronal avalanches, self-organized criticality, and emergent dynamics.

Dynamical SystemsBrian2
No. 04

History of Complex Numbers in Physics

A general-audience talk tracing how complex numbers entered physics — connecting the history of science, mathematics, and physical theory for listeners with no technical background.
